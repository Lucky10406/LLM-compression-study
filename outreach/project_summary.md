# Project summary

This study evaluates whether a QLoRA-fine-tuned Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct model can be made more deployment-efficient for 10-class HumAID disaster-tweet classification without losing useful predictive quality. I compared FP16 inference with INT8 and INT4 NF4 quantization, 30/50/70% one-shot unstructured magnitude pruning, and a task-specific DistilBERT student trained with Qwen-derived soft targets. INT4 NF4 was the strongest tested Qwen quality-memory option, reaching 0.7565 accuracy and 0.7497 macro F1 while reducing recorded peak GPU memory by 65.77% relative to FP16. Naive pruning showed a sharp failure boundary between 30% and 50% sparsity. The distilled 66.96M-parameter classifier reached 0.7735 accuracy and 0.7630 macro F1 with about 4.38 ms median latency. Energy and emissions are CodeCarbon estimates, and conclusions remain limited to this dataset, benchmark, and T4 environment.
